# Narrative-First Review Workflow

ScholarPen defaults to narrative/thematic evidence synthesis. PRISMA 2020 reporting, explicit research questions, database-specific searches, deduplication, title/abstract/metadata screening, eligibility criteria, audit trail, study characteristics, limitations, references, and evidence-based conclusions are core workflow elements.

Formal meta-analysis, forest plots, GRADE, sophisticated risk-of-bias tools, elaborate evidence hierarchies, computational benchmarking, exaggerated impact language, and elaborate quality scoring are optional compatibility capabilities only; they are not forced or automatically generated. Quantitative pooling requires explicit comparable effect data and an appropriate user-selected method.

Every final included record must be carried through synthesis. If context limits require multiple passes, use bounded per-record representations or chunking and reconcile all included record IDs. Never use a first-N subset as the final synthesis evidence base. The 100+100 pools are processing aids only and do not cap synthesis coverage.

Manuscript study tables contain included papers only. Conclusions are synthesized from the complete included evidence and are rendered without in-text citations or citation markers in both preview and exports.
